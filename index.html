03 — Chess variants

When 64 squares aren't strange enough.

Every variant, unlimited, for everyone. Each has its own rating, its own leaderboard, and its own way of breaking your brain.

Chess960

Fischer Random: back-rank pieces shuffled into one of 960 mirrored setups — bishops on opposite colors, king between the rooks. Castling lands on the normal destination squares.

King of the Hill

Normal chess — but march your king to d4, d5, e4 or e5 and you win instantly, whatever else is happening on the board.

Three-Check

Deliver check three times and the game is over. Checkmate still wins too, but why wait?

Crazyhouse

Captured pieces join your bank. Instead of moving, drop one on any empty square — even with check. Pawns can't drop on the 1st or 8th rank.

Bughouse

Two boards, four players, two teams. Your captures go to your partner's hand on the other board. The match ends the instant either board does.

Atomic

Every capture explodes, vaporizing the capturer and all non-pawns on the 8 surrounding squares. Kings can't capture. Blow up the enemy king to win.

Horde

White: 36 pawns, nothing else. Black: a normal army. White mates the king; Black must eat every last pawn.

Antichess

Captures are mandatory when available. Lose all your pieces (or get stalemated) to win. Pawns may promote to kings. No check, no castling — no mercy.

Racing Kings

No pawns; everyone starts on ranks 1–2. First king to the 8th rank wins. Giving check is illegal — this is a footrace, not a fistfight.

Fog of War

You only see squares your pieces could legally move to. No check or checkmate — the game ends when a king is actually captured. Good luck out there.

4-Player Chess

Four armies on a cross-shaped board. Solo (only 1st wins), Free-For-All (top two win), or Teams with your diagonal partner.

Seasonal & niche

No-Castling, Torpedo, Sideways Pawns, Capture Anything, Duck Chess, Spell Chess and more rotate in and out of events — check the Variants page for what's live.

Note: the rated-tournament line-up changes over time; some variants are periodically added or removed from rated play.
