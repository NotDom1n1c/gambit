    Under the hood

    A chess engine, written from scratch.

    • Move generatorChecked with perft against the known node counts, including castling, en passant and promotion.
    • SearchAlpha-beta with quiescence search and move ordering; each bot gets its own depth and randomness.
    • EvaluationMaterial, piece-square tables and a mop-up term that drives a lone king to the edge.
    • Local onlyEverything runs in your browser. Games, ratings and settings stay in local storage on your device.
